Make-up for Beginners!

Welcometo Make-up for Beginners! On this page you will find the essential products to creat a flawless look. To make things easy to follow, the products are organised in four catogories.Whether you are completely new to make-up or simply want to improve your skills, this guide will help you get started.

Base

Base is the most important step when it comes to a fresh make-up look. If the base is to oily or to cracky all the other steps will be pointless. The products used to creat a smooth base are:

  1. Moisteriser
  2. Primer
  3. Foundation
  4. Concealer
  5. Setting Powder
  6. Setting Spray

Eyes

Eye makeup can be done in various styles according to the event. Here we will focus on the accential products needed for a everyday look.

  • Nude Eyeshadow Pallet
  • Eyeliner
  • Eyepencil
  • Eyebrow Pencil
  • Mascara

Cheeks

Cheek Makeup is mostly based on personal preferences. Some people go for a bold look by using dark pink shades while others go for a more natural look using rosy pink colours.

To add warmth and sculp the face some people use bronzer and highliter.

lips

Lip colour should be used according to your lip size. Someone with small lips can use lighter colours and add some gloss on top og the lipstick. If someone has fuller lips they are free in using any colour. To give your lips a defined shape a lip pencil is used in ashde darker then the lipstick.
